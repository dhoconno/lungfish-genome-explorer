OVERVIEW: Export FASTQ views and tables with provenance

USAGE: lungfish-cli fastq export <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  sample-barcodes         Write the sample barcode sheet of the FASTQ metadata
                          drawer as CSV with provenance

  See 'lungfish-cli help fastq export <subcommand>' for detailed help.
