OVERVIEW: Design single, tiled, or qPCR schemes with varVAMP through the
versioned LGE adapter

The cumulative consensus threshold is passed directly to native -t and is never
complemented. Single/tiled bounds become --opt-length and --max-length; qPCR
bounds become QAMPLICON_LENGTH. The nominal size is retained for display and
provenance.

USAGE: lungfish-cli primers design varvamp [<options>] --output <output>

OPTIONS:
  --msa <msa>
  --output <output>
  --mode <mode>           single, tiled, or qpcr (default: tiled)
  --grouping <grouping>   varVAMP supports independent only (default:
                          independent)
  --python-path <python-path>
  --amplicon-size <amplicon-size>
                          Nominal display/provenance size. Defaults to 400 for
                          single/tiled and 135 for qpcr.
  --amplicon-size-min <amplicon-size-min>
                          Inclusive minimum span including primer sites;
                          defaults to 90% of nominal for single/tiled and to
                          varVAMP's own qPCR minimum of 70 for qpcr.
  --amplicon-size-max <amplicon-size-max>
                          Inclusive maximum span including primer sites;
                          defaults to 110% of nominal for single/tiled and to
                          varVAMP's own qPCR maximum of 200 for qpcr.
  --workers <workers>     (default: 4)
  --consensus-threshold <consensus-threshold>
  --maximum-primer-ambiguities <maximum-primer-ambiguities>
                          (default: 2)
  --maximum-probe-ambiguities <maximum-probe-ambiguities>
  --tiled-overlap <tiled-overlap>
                          (default: 25)
  --report-count <report-count>
  --qpcr-test-count <qpcr-test-count>
                          (default: 50)
  --qpcr-delta-g <qpcr-delta-g>
                          (default: -3)
  --scheme-name <scheme-name>
                          (default: varVAMP)
  --compatible-primers <compatible-primers>
  --blast-database <blast-database>
  --screen-against <screen-against>
                          Sequences to screen candidate oligos against: a
                          .lungfishmsa (ungapped rows), .lungfishref, or
                          nucleotide FASTA. LGE builds the BLAST database with
                          makeblastdb. Repeatable; mutually exclusive with
                          --blast-database.
  --terminal-masking-threshold <terminal-masking-threshold>
  --primer-tm-min <primer-tm-min>
  --primer-tm-opt <primer-tm-opt>
  --primer-tm-max <primer-tm-max>
  --primer-gc-min <primer-gc-min>
  --primer-gc-opt <primer-gc-opt>
  --primer-gc-max <primer-gc-max>
  --primer-size-min <primer-size-min>
  --primer-size-opt <primer-size-opt>
  --primer-size-max <primer-size-max>
  --primer-maximum-poly-x <primer-maximum-poly-x>
  --primer-maximum-dinucleotide-repeats <primer-maximum-dinucleotide-repeats>
  --primer-hairpin <primer-hairpin>
  --primer-gc-end-min <primer-gc-end-min>
  --primer-gc-end-max <primer-gc-end-max>
  --primer-minimum-3-prime-without-ambiguity <primer-minimum-3-prime-without-ambiguity>
  --primer-maximum-dimer-temperature <primer-maximum-dimer-temperature>
  --primer-maximum-dimer-delta-g <primer-maximum-dimer-delta-g>
  --end-overlap <end-overlap>
  --probe-tm-min <probe-tm-min>
  --probe-tm-opt <probe-tm-opt>
  --probe-tm-max <probe-tm-max>
  --probe-size-min <probe-size-min>
  --probe-size-opt <probe-size-opt>
  --probe-size-max <probe-size-max>
  --probe-gc-min <probe-gc-min>
  --probe-gc-opt <probe-gc-opt>
  --probe-gc-max <probe-gc-max>
  --probe-gc-end-min <probe-gc-end-min>
  --probe-gc-end-max <probe-gc-end-max>
  --qprimer-difference <qprimer-difference>
  --probe-temperature-difference-min <probe-temperature-difference-min>
  --probe-temperature-difference-max <probe-temperature-difference-max>
  --probe-distance-min <probe-distance-min>
  --probe-distance-max <probe-distance-max>
  --amplicon-gc-min <amplicon-gc-min>
  --amplicon-gc-max <amplicon-gc-max>
  --amplicon-deletion-cutoff <amplicon-deletion-cutoff>
  --pcr-monovalent-concentration <pcr-monovalent-concentration>
  --pcr-divalent-concentration <pcr-divalent-concentration>
  --pcr-dntp-concentration <pcr-dntp-concentration>
  --pcr-dna-concentration <pcr-dna-concentration>
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
