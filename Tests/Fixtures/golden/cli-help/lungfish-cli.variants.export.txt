OVERVIEW: Export variant drawer tables with provenance

USAGE: lungfish-cli variants export <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  bookmarked              Write the bookmarked variants of variant databases as
                          TSV with provenance
  sample-template         Write a sample metadata template of a bundle's
                          variant samples as TSV or CSV with provenance

  See 'lungfish-cli help variants export <subcommand>' for detailed help.
