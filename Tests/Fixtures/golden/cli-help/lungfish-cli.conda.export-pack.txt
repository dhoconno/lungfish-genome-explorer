OVERVIEW: Export installed conda environments for offline transfer

USAGE: lungfish-cli conda export-pack --pack <pack> --output <output> [--conda-root <conda-root>]

OPTIONS:
  --pack <pack>           Built-in tool pack ID to export
  -o, --output <output>   Offline pack output directory, .tar, .tgz, or .tar.gz
                          archive
  --conda-root <conda-root>
                          Conda root to export from (default: managed storage
                          conda root)
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
