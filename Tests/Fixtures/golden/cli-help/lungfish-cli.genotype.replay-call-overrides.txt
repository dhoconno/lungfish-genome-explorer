OVERVIEW: Replay a recorded atomic haplotype call override edit into its exact
target bundle

USAGE: lungfish-cli genotype replay-call-overrides --provenance <provenance> --bundle <bundle>

OPTIONS:
  --provenance <provenance>
                          GUI annotation provenance containing the replay
                          payload
  --bundle <bundle>       Exact genotype result bundle recorded by the replay
                          payload
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
