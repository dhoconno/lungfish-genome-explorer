OVERVIEW: Inspect provenance recorded in Lungfish bundles

USAGE: lungfish-cli provenance <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  bibliography            Generate a citation list from a bundle's provenance
  export                  Export reproducible reports from a Lungfish
                          provenance sidecar
  verify                  Verify a signed Lungfish provenance sidecar

  See 'lungfish-cli help provenance <subcommand>' for detailed help.
