Classifier results are candidate evidence. Treat a call as stronger when read support, coverage, identity, and independent verification agree.

Review Steps

  1. Open the classifier result.
  2. Filter by sample, organism, or accession.
  3. Compare total reads, unique reads, coverage, and identity.
  4. Use BLAST Verify or Extract FASTQ when a selected call needs review.

Interpretation

Reads alone can overstate evidence when duplicates or multi-mapping are present. Broad coverage and unique reads usually support a call more strongly.
