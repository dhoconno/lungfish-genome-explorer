FASTQ workflows can trim, filter, demultiplex, map, assemble, or extract reads. Check inputs, settings, output strategy, and readiness before running.

Using a Workflow Dialog

  1. Select FASTQ or FASTA inputs.
  2. Choose the operation that matches the biological question.
  3. Confirm required references, databases, and barcode files.
  4. Choose the output strategy and click Run.

Provenance

Data-changing read workflows write provenance for commands, tool versions, defaults, inputs, checksums, status, outputs, and runtime.
