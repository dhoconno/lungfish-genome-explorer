Welcome Reads and Workflows Classification Review Provenance

Alignment and variant workflows turn reads into mapped evidence and candidate variants. Reference choice, BAM track, primer scheme, caller, thresholds, and model all affect results.

Review Steps

  1. Select an analysis-ready BAM track.
  2. Confirm primer trimming when the caller requires it.
  3. Choose caller thresholds or leave fields blank for defaults.
  4. Inspect the resulting VCF or variant track.

Provenance

Variant outputs should record the command, caller version, reference, BAM track, model, thresholds, inputs, checksums, status, and runtime.
