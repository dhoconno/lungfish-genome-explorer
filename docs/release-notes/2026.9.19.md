## Excel views that match LGE

Excel exports now include complete effective H1/H2 calls, including homozygous calls and exact DR/DRB, DQ, and DP locus identities. Filtered exports use the active calls displayed by LGE, including recomputation after evidence reviews, rather than an older saved analysis. Raw baselines and explicit missing or unresolved calls remain distinct.

The filtered workbook contains the visible Genotype Matrix, Haplotype Calls for the captured sample/locus scope, and Export Metadata. It preserves projected values, order, visible false-positive/false-negative annotations, comments, and display colors without retaining unfiltered companion sheets. Valid filter drafts are committed before capture, and the same snapshot is used through the export dialogs. Percent thresholds and their denominators are recorded together, with configured but inactive row-support filters identified correctly.

Visual filters do not change haplotype inference. The editable current.xlsx retains all evidence and the complete exact-locus call set, including loci hidden by display filters.

## One Inspector export entry point

Use **Export to Excel…** in the Inspector to choose:

- **Filtered view** for a one-way copy of the captured LGE view. Changes to this file do not return to LGE.
- **Editable workbook — current.xlsx** for full-evidence review and supported Excel edits.

The dialog explains each role and displays the captured filters and scope. The same Inspector section shows workbook status, the last filtered export, and **Review Excel Changes…** when needed. Unavailable editing actions explain the write-access requirement. Confirm and Skip remain limited to the explicit Review workflow.

## Reviewed Excel edits

Use the **Edit Calls** and **Edit Matrix** sheets for supported H1/H2 overrides, false-positive/false-negative review decisions, and comments. Keep, set, and clear are explicit operations; raw evidence is read-only. Review the proposed identities and before/after values in LGE before importing. A formatting-only save uses **Acknowledge & Refresh**.

Accepted changes save as one batch, refresh LGE, and regenerate current.xlsx. The edited workbook and reproducibility evidence are retained. Modified scientific evidence, ambiguous identities, stale inputs, and concurrent external edits are rejected without partial application or silently overwriting unreviewed changes. Excel operations remain tied to their originating project and window.

Legacy manual-only haplotype assignments and calls without raw baselines remain read-only in Excel; supported matrix reviews and comments remain available. Use LGE for those legacy call edits.
