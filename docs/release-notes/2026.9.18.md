# Lungfish 2026.9.18

Channel: Preview

Previous versioned release: v2026.9.17

Stable baseline: v2026.9.4

Dependency set: 2026.2

Preview builds are under rapid iterative development. Features may be incomplete, change quickly, or require additional feedback.

## MHC haplotype corrections

This release corrects MHC workflow regressions reported in Preview 2026.9.17. Default ordering now keeps a coherent intact M-family in H1 across informative loci, ahead of a chromosome composed of different M-family regions. When both families are intact, the lower numbered family takes H1. Explicit manual edits and per-locus swaps remain authoritative.

Marking an observed genotype as false positive immediately recomputes deterministic haplotype calls and their evidence display. Clearing the review restores that evidence. Reopening a project uses saved reviews, including older projects whose matching haplotype definition must be recovered from their recorded reference bundle. Raw reads are preserved; false-negative annotations do not fabricate supporting reads. Scientific percentage thresholds retain their documented percent units.

A call changes only when the remaining evidence no longer satisfies its reference diagnostics. Reviewing one allele does not exclude a different diagnostic allele automatically. Viewport visibility filters do not change haplotype inference.

## Filtered Excel export

Filtered Pivot recognizes both original and updated workbook layouts, including the Full Sequencing Results sheet, and resolves compact allele labels without silently accepting ambiguous identities. The export retains visible sample/allele order, projected cell values, false-positive and false-negative annotations, and native Excel comments. Recognized haplotype header rows reflect current calls and manual overrides rather than stale workbook values. Reproducibility provenance remains attached to the export.

Existing projects do not require data repair. Reopen them to refresh deterministic calls; saved manual haplotype assignments are preserved. Update the current workbook when prompted to publish changed calls into that workbook.

## Simpler haplotype browsing

Confirm and Skip are hidden during ordinary Haplotype Calls browsing. They remain in the explicit Review view, where Confirm records review status and advances the queue and Skip moves to the next review item. Haplotype edits and swaps continue to save immediately.

## Dependency versions

Dependency set 2026.2 and managed tool version pins are unchanged.
