# Lungfish 2026.11.4

Channel: Preview

Previous versioned release: v2026.11.3

Stable baseline: v2026.10.29

Dependency set: 2026.2

Preview builds are under rapid iterative development. Features may be incomplete, change quickly, or require additional feedback.

This Preview shows a result you choose with an accessibility tool in the viewer, and it has Demultiplex Barcodes say when a barcode not in your pool could pass as a sample.

## What's new

- **A result you choose with an accessibility tool now shows in the viewer.** Before, when a result viewer such as the TaxTriage table had the keyboard focus, choosing another result in the sidebar with an accessibility tool, such as the Accessibility Inspector or an app that drives Lungfish for you, sent the sidebar back to the result on screen and said nothing. Now the viewer, the Inspector and the sidebar all show the result you chose.
- **Demultiplex Barcodes says when a barcode not in your pool could pass.** When a barcode that is not in your pool could pass as a sample, Demultiplex Barcodes now says so in its summary and in the Operations Panel. The line itself is under Things to know.

## Things to know

- **A barcode not in your pool.** Demultiplex Barcodes can still pass such a barcode as a sample, in about one run in 1,000 for each one and more often in amplicon files, and says so in the Operations Panel and in the summary of the command line. Check samples with few reads against your sample sheet.
- **Earlier notes.** The limits in the 2026.11.3 notes still apply.

## Updating

Choose Check for Updates… in the app menu to install this release. It adds no tool to install on the Mac.
