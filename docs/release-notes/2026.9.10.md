Channel: Preview

Preview builds are under rapid iterative development. Features may be incomplete, change quickly, or require additional feedback.

## Multiple sequence alignment viewer

Alignments fit the available viewport after opening and resizing. Manual zoom keeps its selected scale until Fit is chosen again.

Select sequence rows by clicking their names, coordinates, or sequence area. Command-click selects discontiguous rows and Shift-click selects a range. Copy and export preserve the selected row subset; aligned output retains gaps. Existing rectangular residue selections remain available.

The generic Annotations, Variants, and Samples table no longer occupies the bottom of the alignment viewer. Available source annotations remain visible on alignment tracks. Source variants and sample metadata are not imported into the alignment.

Use a row's **Use as Reference** action to compare other sequences against it. The reference remains readable and visibly marked, and the Inspector reflects the chosen reference. This changes the display without changing the alignment or calling variants.

Clipboard alignment exports retain the exported payload and its reproducibility record in application support storage. The clipboard includes plain text, the retained file location, and provenance with the exact selection and output identity.

## Dependencies

Dependency set 2026.2 and managed tool version pins are unchanged.
