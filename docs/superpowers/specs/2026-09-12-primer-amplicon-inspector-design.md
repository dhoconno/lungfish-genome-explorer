# Primer selection and Inspector integration

Overview and Results share a saved target, amplicon and optional individual-primer selection. Pool lanes make overlapping schemes distinguishable. Clicking an amplicon shows its span and pool with every associated oligo; clicking a primer keeps that individual oligo highlighted within its amplicon. Result-list selections scroll the design into view. A selected native primer can open its exact mapped site in the existing read-only MSA inspection. Primer3 candidate lists adapt to narrow viewports.

Native PrimalScheme membership uses the exact result, reference, amplicon name and pool. The amplicon BED name is retained. Primer names encode the amplicon identity and independent LEFT/RIGHT alternative indices; equal alternative indices are not treated as matched pairs. Duplicate, incomplete, wrong-pool, wrong-strand or inconsistent-envelope records remain unlinked and visibly unavailable. Authentic native amplicon spans enclose the forward and reverse clouds, including primer sequences. Their displayed length is a reference span, not a claim about every alternative product or allele. Primer3 uses explicit pair/probe membership and the product size on the saved template, with no pool assignment.

The viewport contains Overview, Results and Binding inspection. Bundle, Files and Provenance use the existing Inspector tab system and styling. The standard ProvenanceSection presents verified wrapper, native-tool, normalization-workflow and derived worksheet envelopes. Source selection bypasses legacy discovery and repair. No schema migration or scientific file write occurs during inspection. Asynchronous handoff checks the original display token and installed controller identity; clearing the viewport removes its Inspector scope. Unrelated content-mode sections do not appear under a primer bundle.

UI/UX, native-format/bioinformatics and Inspector architecture reviewers checked the implementation. Native source and 23 saved human/macaque output sets established identifier and envelope semantics, including 177 groups with independent alternatives. The integrated 97-test suite passed, covering model membership, probes, cross-window routing, teardown, read-only source switching, operation lifecycle regressions, saved MHC analyses and offscreen views. Wide/narrow selected-result views, native MHC results, alignment inspection and standard Inspector tabs were visually checked. Evidence remains in `.build/primer-amplicon-green-tests.log` and `.build/primer-amplicon-visual/` in the isolated worktree.
