# Measurements behind the phase-session rules

This file holds the measurements behind the rules of `docs/contracts/RUNNING-A-PHASE-SESSION.md`, one row a rule, with
the sessions that measured each. That contract keeps the session table and the meaning of direct and all-in cost, and
its End-of-session duty adds each session's measurements here. Lane 12.42 of 2.12 moved the ledger to the price table of
2026-10-18 between the two sessions of 2.12. A row whose Sessions cell says old table gives its dollars on the table
before it, except the sentences marked as session 2 of 2.12 or the new table. On session 1 of 2.12 the new table lowered
sonnet dollars about 19% and raised opus dollars 1.2%, and 2% to 18% on small sessions, so a figure that mixes the two
models has no single factor.
