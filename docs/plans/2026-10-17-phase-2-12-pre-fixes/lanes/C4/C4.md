It calls `ProvenanceRecorder.findProvenanceReading(for:)`, then `ProvenanceLineageResolver.resolve(envelope:sidecarURL:sourceRootURL:)` with the default shared `ProvenanceSidecarIndex`. An observer on the resolver counts `Event.indexedSidecar` (sidecars read by the index), and a `BundleLoadProbe` observer lists manifest decodes during a walk. Footprint is `task_info` `phys_footprint` sampled every 20 ms, and the process' `ledger_phys_footprint_peak` is the cross-check.

### C4 decision (ARCH-perf.md line 166)

The first walk takes 33.5 s as landed and 57.3 s with every sidecar read, both under 60 s. The footprint peaks at 129 MB and 850 MB for the walk, and at 194 MB and 941 MB for the process, far under 2 GB. **By the rule, the persistent sidecar index moves out to Phase 3. It is not a Phase 2.12 lane.** The P1 review's own 5 minute rule for opening the persisted index is not met either.

Two things the manager should weigh before closing it.

1. The cap. `ProvenanceSidecarIndex.candidates(in:maximumFiles:)` stops after 4,000 enumerated entries (files and folders), and project 32616 has 9,330. As landed the index holds 282 of 1,390 sidecars, so about four in five are invisible to a checksum lookup (BIO 6). A producer past the cap ends the lineage early. That did not happen for the two items walked here. If the cap is raised to fix that, the first walk is the 57.3 s of run B, only 2.7 s under the line on this Mac with CPU time of 40.8 s. On a slower Mac it crosses 60 s, and the persistent index question opens again.
2. The load. Run A's wall time was inflated by a machine that was far from idle. I would not quote 33.5 s as the unloaded figure. Run B ran at light load and its wall time is closer to the CPU time.

Not run. The P1 fix 9 check that five EsViritu sidecars read the same through `ProvenanceEnvelopeReader.load(fromSidecar:)` and `producers(ofChecksum:)`. It is not in the C4 brief, and the lineages walked here hold no EsViritu record.
