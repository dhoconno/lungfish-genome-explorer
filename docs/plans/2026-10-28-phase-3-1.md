# Phase 3.1, the quality net, then the classifier core

This plan is active. It is sub-phase 3.1 of the Phase 3 plan in `docs/plans/2026-10-02-architecture-program.md`
(`## Phase 3`), under `docs/contracts/SCOPE-AND-DELIVERY.md`. The audit of 2026-10-26 planned it, so no inventory, memo or
start vote runs. ARCH reads this plan before the first lane. Records, briefs, rulings, reviews and lane reports are in
`~/lungfish-phase-records/phase-3-1/` (SCR below).

## Outcome and budget

A lab on Stable switches between classifier results without a stale or blank viewer, reads plain words that name the
failed step and the next thing to try when a run fails, and can cite the database release behind EsViritu, TaxTriage
and NAO-MGS calls. The four result defects that Stable 2026.10.27 shipped as latent or rare are fixed and reach Stable
within seven days of landing. The six classifier viewers open through one `ViewportHost` and share one action component.
A Stable release from `v2026.10.27`'s successor ends 3.1, due by 2026-11-11 under the Stable clock.

Budget two sessions and about $2,500 measured, about $1,250 for session 1. The budget is soft (O78). At $1,875 in session
1, and at $3,750 for the sub-phase, the manager starts no new lane and asks the owner.

The HOST lane builds on NET's head, so the switch matrix of every pair of kinds is frozen at its base with today's leaks
declared, and each migration removes its kind's declared leaks. The migrations share `ViewerViewController.swift`, so
they run one at a time, each one revertible range closed by its view-switch test and a short walk.

At most five building agents run at once. The first wave is NET (the longest chain), NVD, MAPPED and TTL2, then LOOP as
soon as QA-RELEASE-LOOP is written. ALERT, DBID, SMOKE and SYMLINK follow as slots free. Lane builds stay on the internal
disk, since stage B of ARCH-SCRATCH needs the build trial and BUILD-LINK-FIND first. Landings are cherry-picks in batches
of three or four reviewed ranges by an `lge-worker` on sonnet at effort max. The `llm-efficiency-monitor` on opus at
effort xhigh runs once, after the last landing and before the final candidate.

## Candidate, walk and release

The final candidate follows `docs/contracts/VERIFICATION-ORDER.md`, as amended by LOOP once it lands. The unit tier
runs once on a quiet Mac with `DatabaseStorageLocation` absent before and after, then the replay suites, `python3
scripts/golden/golden.py compare` and the executor plan replay with no skip. `release.py package preview` makes the
candidate, and the XCUI smoke and the view-switch tests run on the packaged app in a session that does not lock. The
release notes are written for bench scientists, under 15,000 characters, with the technical record and the owner check
list as separate files in `docs/reports/`.

The walk covers every changed screen and the six open rows of `docs/reports/2026.10.27-owner-checks.md`, with the owner
at galah on 2026-10-29 (O84). Each finding gets a severity, S1 and S2 are fixed, and only the changed screens are walked
again. On the owner's go `main` is pushed by fast-forward, `release.py setup` runs right before `release.py publish`,
and the Preview is published (O85). Session 2 finishes the migrations and the Stable.
