each sample by that index, or a refusal in words, and never barcodes given by chance. The Demux tab's plan reaches the run with the
sample names typed in the drawer, and Detect Barcodes counts the read-name index. The packaged smoke gates the Stable from a checked
copy outside `~/Documents`. The first monthly Stable after Preview 2026.11.2 carries 3.2's and 3.3's work, as the panel's D2 sets.

Phase 3's $9,000 line stops work. Quality and reasonableness lead (O78), and the quota pacing of
`docs/contracts/RUNNING-A-PHASE-SESSION.md` holds. 3.3 is one session and ends in a Preview.
