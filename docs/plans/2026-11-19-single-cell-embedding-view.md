## User outcome

A bench scientist opens a single-cell result and sees its cells on the UMAP, t-SNE or PHATE picture that Seurat, Scanpy or Cell Ranger made. The cells can be coloured by cluster, sample, the per-cell totals, the not-host columns or one gene. `lungfish-cli` writes every value, count and legend bound, and the view only reads them. The scientist selects cells by lasso or from the legend and pseudobulks the selection, and the record names how the cells were chosen. `lungfish-cli` also computes the clusters and a UMAP by a pinned Scanpy route whose output equals its reproduction script byte for byte, and the view shows them. A Seurat object exported from a result with two or more samples keeps those samples apart. The 5.5 Preview carries all of it after a packaged walk of the changed screens (X6).
