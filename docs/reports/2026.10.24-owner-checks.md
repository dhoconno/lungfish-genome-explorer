## Checks still to run

This list holds the rows that gate a Stable promotion. The first part carries every row of the 2026.10.23 list that the panel did not rule Pass after the re-walk of 2026-10-24 (`docs/reports/2026.10.23-owner-checks.md`, "After the re-walk of 2026-10-24"), with what is left for each. Every permission that the walk needs, such as computer use, the Finder grant or a privacy prompt, is the owner's own click. By the owner's direction, screenshot recaptures and manual rewrites wait until every phase has ended.
