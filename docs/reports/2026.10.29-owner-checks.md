# Lungfish 2026.10.29 owner check list

This is the owner check list of Stable 2026.10.29. It carries the five rows of Preview 2026.10.28 that the walks did not run, adds the walk steps that the reviews and lane reports of session 2 wrote for each changed screen, and adds the BLAST Verify rerun that the ruling BIO-BLAST-CLADE asks for and the XCUI smoke on the packaged candidate. It holds at most 27 rows still to run, and rows 18 to 24 cover the saved BLAST results, the exported table's taxon columns, the Operations words, a 12S result replaced on disk, an assembly result that was moved or copied and an assembly whose contigs changed on disk. Rows 7 and 8b replace the BLAST Verify rerun after that rerun failed on candidate c7ec1c761 on 2026-11-01. Each row is one task that no automated test covers, and a row that a test comes to cover is deleted. A failed row is a finding with a severity, and an open S1 or S2 finding blocks this Stable release. Every permission that the walk needs, such as computer use, the Finder grant or a privacy prompt, is the owner's own click.

The Stable walk runs with the owner at the Mac Studio after the candidate gates and the packaged smoke (O106 says to assume the owner is there whenever the candidate is ready). `SCR` is `/Users/dho/lungfish-phase-records/phase-3-1`. The steps of each new row are in the review or lane report that the Script column names, under `SCR/reviews` and `SCR/lanes`, and the steps of the carried rows are in `/Users/dho/lungfish-phase-records/phase-3-0/walk/REWALK.md`. Severity follows `docs/contracts/SCOPE-AND-DELIVERY.md`. S1 is a false number, a silently wrong result, silent data loss or a crash. S2 is a common task that fails or a failure that shows no words. S3 is a rare task that fails or words that mislead. S4 is appearance.

Work on a copy of a project, never on the only copy of a result. A row that sends reads to NCBI, which is every BLAST run, starts only on the owner's go in chat. A row that names an old NAO-MGS result needs one imported by Stable 2026.10.27 or earlier, with the results file it came from.
