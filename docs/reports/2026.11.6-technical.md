## The release

Preview 2026.11.6 is the Preview that closes Phase 4 (`docs/plans/2026-11-09-phase-4.md`). The review of 2026-11-11 (`docs/reports/2026-11-11-program-review/SUMMARY.md`) closed Phase 4 at this Preview and dropped its session 2. Owner direction O159 says no stable release is built in this phase unless the owner asks, and the owner asked for one (O161), so the stable release 2026.11.7 follows this Preview by the steps of `docs/contracts/VERIFICATION-ORDER.md`. Preview 2026.11.6 builds on Preview 2026.11.5 (build 12859 from `a46e89605`) and on `origin/main` at `77a13f511`, the head the phase started from. `SCR` is `/Users/dho/lungfish-phase-records/phase-4`.

A person sees twelve changes, and the notes list them. Three were wrong numbers or silent faults. A Kraken 2 sample with no total of its own showed the batch's total and unclassified count as its own (KRAKEN-TOTAL-FALLBACK, with KRAKEN-TOOLTIP-ZERO-PERCENT for the 0% in the hover card, lane RIDERS-RESULT). An exported figure counted depth under no cutoff while the window counted it under its mapping-quality and base-quality cutoffs, so the figure's max, mean and covered share could be larger (EXPORT-DEPTH-FILTERS, S2 by BIO, lane FIGURE-DEPTH). A TaxTriage result's database could record another sample's BAM file for a sample whose own file was missing (BUILDDB-BAM-FIT, S1 by effect, found by the walk and fixed in round 2). Two more fixes close S2 findings of the probe of the 2026.11.5 package. Demultiplex Barcodes on a derived bundle recorded a temporary copy that the run had deleted and a command that could not run (DEMUX-STAGED-INPUT), and a subset of ONT reads lost its platform, so Demultiplex Barcodes, Map and Assemble could start on short-read settings (DERIVED-PLATFORM).

The other changes are words and behaviour. The NAO-MGS Taxa count counts each taxon once (NAOMGS-TAXA-COUNT-WORDS). The ONT folder import sheet counts the barcode folders and says what it leaves out (ONT-WORDS). The Results button finds a BLAST search saved inside a Kraken 2 result (OPS-RESULTS-BLAST-ANCESTOR, finished in round 2). The mapped contig list keeps its zoom after a sort or a filter (RM-LIST-VIEWPORT). An exported figure says what it leaves out (ALIGN-IMAGE-EXPORT-MASKING and W7-COVERAGE-TIER-FACT). Extract Reads says which BAM file it read (the scan sentence of RIDERS-RESULT, extended in round 2 to the database route).

Phase 4's moves change nothing a person sees. L4D-PILOT lifts the TwelveS folder into its own target `LungfishWorkflowTwelveS` (`446de1d4f`). L4B-DRAWER splits `AnnotationTableDrawerView.swift` into the file and six extension files, from 5,619 lines to 2,394. MATRIX-SPLIT-1 moves the six cell view classes of the genotype matrix to `GenotypeMatrixCellViews.swift`, and the matrix view file goes from 9,319 to 8,540 lines. L4C-PIN pins coverage across spliced and deleted reads to `samtools depth`. KIT adds the tools that prove a move (`verify_pure_move.py`, `file-size.sh --renames` and `test_set_diff.py`), and MEASURE-BEFORE and MEASURE-AFTER record the build times.

It holds the 82 commits of the program branch `phase-4` between `77a13f511` and `a8384152dffa42391bbc47b14d6689edbc1ac1b8` (P2), counted with `git rev-list --count 77a13f511..a8384152d`, and no merge commit exists.
