# Lungfish 2026.11.5 owner check list

This is the owner check list of Preview 2026.11.5. It holds the checks that need a person's eyes, ears or hands, or a walk of the packaged build that no automated test covers. The walk results and the publish facts follow in a later commit. Until then each row says who walks it and how, and a row that waits for the owner says so.

It is the list of Preview 2026.11.4 (`docs/reports/2026.11.4-owner-checks.md`) with the changes of the Phase 3.4 plan (`docs/plans/2026-11-08-phase-3-4.md`, decisions B, B1, C5 and C8 and the section The walk). Row 24 left the list, since UX ruled its finding S4 by design. Rows 23 and 25 are rewritten for the new behavior and are walked in this Preview's walk, with row 26 and way (c) of row 9. Row 13 carries the words that close decision B. Row 20 is the console run of the XCUI twin of old row 35, now unattended, and its command names the variable `LUNGFISH_RELEASE_SMOKE_FIXTURES`. Row 21 stays open until the smoke result of `ExactBareWordsXCUITests` on the package reads green. Old row 8 passes on a test, old row 9 is walked, and old row 10 stays with the owner for its PARK half. The rows of the older lists 2026.11.1, 2026.11.2 and 2026.11.3 that this list still carries hold their Do, Passes and Fails words here, in the section Do, Passes and Fails of the older rows (decision D4), so that those three lists can close. Rows 1 to 4 are the rows that need the owner's own senses or accounts. Each row is one task, and a row that a test comes to cover is deleted. Owner rows do not block the Preview. The owner's direction O159 (`docs/reports/2026.11.4-technical.md`, Panel decisions) runs the walk of this Preview in background control without the owner, so a row that needs full-screen control or the owner says that it waits for the owner. A failed row is a finding with a severity, and an open S1 or S2 finding blocks the next stable release. Every permission that the walk needs, such as computer use, the Finder grant or a privacy prompt, is the owner's own click.

`SCR` is `/Users/dho/lungfish-phase-records/phase-3-2`. The owner works through VNC and hears no audio (O116), so a row about sound waits for the owner at the Mac Studio itself, and the manager otherwise reads VoiceOver's caption panel and says so. Rows 5 to 20 need a context menu, a window set to an exact width, a click on screen or a Terminal command, so the manager walks them by computer use with the owner at galah. The walk needs an unlocked screen, and the walk copy is installed and run outside `~/Documents` (`~/LGE-GUI-Walk/<date>/candidate`). Severity follows `docs/contracts/SCOPE-AND-DELIVERY.md`. S1 is a false number, a silently wrong result, silent data loss or a crash. S2 is a common task that fails or a failure that shows no words. S3 is a rare task that fails or words that mislead. S4 is appearance.

Work on a copy of a project, never on the only copy of a result. Rows 10 and 13 send BLAST searches to the recorded-reply stand-in on 127.0.0.1 only, so nothing reaches NCBI. A row that sends reads to NCBI starts only on the owner's go in chat or on the standing go's named list (O126), and no BLAST probe runs.

The line for Extract Reads on a raw TaxTriage result rests on the prefix tests of `TaxTriageRecordedBAMTests` and on no row.
