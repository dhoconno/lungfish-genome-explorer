# Lungfish 2026.11.4 owner check list

This is the owner check list of Preview 2026.11.4. It holds the checks that need a person's eyes, ears or hands, or a walk of the packaged build that no automated test covers. It is the list of Preview 2026.11.3 (`docs/reports/2026.11.3-owner-checks.md`) with row 9 rewritten for the accessibility select fix (FOCUSAX), a note on row 21, and a new row 22 for the not-in-pool line of Demultiplex Barcodes (DECOYWORDS). Row 5 is old row 11, in the manager's recommendation of D6. Rows 6 to 8 come from the reviews of the focus fix (FOCUSDROP), row 9 comes from the review of its follow-up (FOCUSAX), rows 10 to 13 come from the report of the OPSWORDS lane, and rows 14 to 17 are the walk rows W1 to W6 that the plan landing (LAND-46) names. Rows 18 and 19 are the Detect rows of LAND-47, with old row 15 rewritten as row 19. Row 20 is the console run of the XCUI twin of old row 35, and row 21 is the Exact Bare step that Walk 33 could not reach (QA advisory 1 of `SCR/reviews/CANDIDATE-57331f67dfb89ed743e1a3abdc8f5194882085b8-QA.md`). Row 22 is the walk row of DECOYWORDS (`SCR/meeting/STB-R2-QA.md:79-90`). Each row is one task, and a row that a test comes to cover is deleted. Owner rows do not block the Preview. A failed row is a finding with a severity, and an open S1 or S2 finding blocks the next Stable release. Every permission that the walk needs, such as computer use, the Finder grant or a privacy prompt, is the owner's own click.

`SCR` is `/Users/dho/lungfish-phase-records/phase-3-2`. The owner works through VNC and hears no audio (O116), so a row about sound waits for the owner at the Mac Studio itself, and the manager otherwise reads VoiceOver's caption panel and says so. Rows 5 to 20 need a context menu, a window set to an exact width, a click on screen or a Terminal command, so the manager walks them by computer use with the owner at galah. The walk needs an unlocked screen, and the walk copy is installed and run outside `~/Documents` (`~/LGE-GUI-Walk/<date>/candidate`). Severity follows `docs/contracts/SCOPE-AND-DELIVERY.md`. S1 is a false number, a silently wrong result, silent data loss or a crash. S2 is a common task that fails or a failure that shows no words. S3 is a rare task that fails or words that mislead. S4 is appearance.

Work on a copy of a project, never on the only copy of a result. Rows 10 and 13 send BLAST searches to the recorded-reply stand-in on 127.0.0.1 only, so nothing reaches NCBI. A row that sends reads to NCBI starts only on the owner's go in chat or on the standing go's named list (O126), and no BLAST probe runs.
