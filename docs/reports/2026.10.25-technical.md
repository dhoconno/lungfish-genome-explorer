In the ruling's runs it did not switch on HiFi-like reads of 300 bases or more, on Illumina scores up to Q41 or on Nanopore scores up to Q50, and the exact score bound between Q51 and Q55 and the exact length bound between 150 and 300 bases were not narrowed. After a switch every later score of the read reads 31 too low. A later score under Q26 stops the tool with exit 1 and a message that blames the reads, a score from Q26 to Q30 is clamped to Q2 and clumpify ends with exit 0, which is silent, and reformat throws `ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException` in a worker thread and never ends, which in LGE lasts until the step's own deadline (`max(900 s, input bytes / 2.5 MB/s)`). The table gives the ruling's measurements, where wrong means a stored score that is not the scheme's map of the input score, `qin` is `qin=33 qout=33` and the ruled flags add `maxcalledquality=93`.

No flag keeps a called Q0 or Q1, which every BBTools tool stores as Q2 (9,056 of 450,000 Nanopore scores in the clump none run), so BIO's ruling puts a sentence on it in the notes. The shipped Preview (`v2026.10.24`) was exposed. `QualityBinningScheme.importDefault` was `illumina4` and `IngestionPlatform.defaultQualityBinning` returned it on every platform (`QualityBinningScheme.swift:29` at the tag), so every platform's default import ran a BBTools storage step with no `qin`. Illumina and Ultima ran clumpify, since clumping defaults on, and Nanopore and PacBio ran reformat, as did the chunks of a Nanopore run folder. Element-like raw reads failed to import with exit 1, quality-filtered short reads at Q55 and above were stored shifted with no error or never finished on reformat, and HiFi reads of 150 bases were stored with about 20% of their scores wrong. Long HiFi reads, Illumina up to Q41 and Nanopore up to Q50 were stored correctly, and with None chosen and clumping on, Element raw reads failed and every HiFi score above Q50 was stored as Q50. The same missing flag sat in the other BBTools calls (deduplicate, the contaminant, entropy, sequence and length filters, primer removal, interleave and split, subsample, repair, error correction and BBMap), where the lane's measurements show exit 1 on most, 20,686 and 20,660 of 45,000 scores silently lowered by the entropy and length filters, and 269,238 of 300,000 by a deduplicate of clean HiFi reads. So BIN does not widen the exposure. The reads at risk are short reads that hold a score of about Q55 or more, such as Element UltraQ-like, short HiFi amplicon and quality-filtered reads, and the results to make again are the imports and BBTools steps on them.
