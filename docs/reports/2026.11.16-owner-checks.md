# Lungfish 2026.11.16 owner check list

This is the owner check list of Preview 2026.11.16. It holds the checks of the info buttons, the cell map and the Demultiplex Barcodes dialog that need your hands or ears, and no automated test covers them. F3 and VoiceOver carry over from the 2026.11.15 list, and the other rows are new. AFF1, MAP1, ASM1 and IMP1 check the Inspector's affected strip and the refusal words of a loose pair in the Map Reads window, the assembly window and the import sheet. Each row is one task of about two minutes (T4 and F3 about three), and a row that a test comes to cover is deleted. The rows block no release. A failed row is a finding with a severity, and an open S1 or S2 finding blocks the next stable release. Severity follows `docs/contracts/SCOPE-AND-DELIVERY.md`. S1 is a false number, a silently wrong result, silent data loss or a crash. S2 is a common task that fails or a failure that shows no words. S3 is a rare task that fails or words that mislead. S4 is appearance.

Work on the copies in the walk folder `~/LGE-GUI-Walk/2026-11-22-5-6`, never on the only copy of a result. Its prep file holds the numbers each row compares with. The Result column holds the answers of the walk and of your run, filled in after them.
