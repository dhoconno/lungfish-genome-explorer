# Lungfish 2026.10.28 owner check list

This is the owner check list of Preview 2026.10.28. It starts from the six rows of Stable 2026.10.27 that were not run before that Stable, and adds the walk steps that the reviews of session 1 wrote for each changed screen. It holds at most 20 rows still to run. Each row is one task that no automated test covers, and a row that a test comes to cover is deleted. A failed row is a finding with a severity, and an open S1 or S2 finding blocks the Stable release that ends Phase 3.1. Every permission that the walk needs, such as computer use, the Finder grant or a privacy prompt, is the owner's own click.

The walk of Preview 2026.10.28 (build 11807, `471167711`) ran on 2026-10-29 with the owner at the Mac Studio (O88), and a second walk followed on the evening of 2026-10-30 (O95 to O98). `SCR` is `/Users/dho/lungfish-phase-records/phase-3-1`, and the log of both walks is `SCR/walk/walk-log.md`. A row that the walks passed in full moved to the section Passed on the walks of 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30 with its result, and a step that failed is written there with its severity and its backlog row. The steps that the walks did not run stay under Checks still to run with only what is left, and they are carried to the pre-Stable walk (O98). The steps of each new row are in the review that the Script column names, under `SCR/reviews`, and the steps of the carried rows are in `/Users/dho/lungfish-phase-records/phase-3-0/walk/REWALK.md`. The walks found no new S1 or S2 in this release. They found one S1 that is older than it, BIO-NAOMGS-BLAST-SCOPE, and its fix must land before the Stable release that ends Phase 3.1.

A row that names an old result needs a NAO-MGS result imported by Stable 2026.10.27 or earlier, with the results file it came from. Work on a copy of a project, never on the only copy of a result.
