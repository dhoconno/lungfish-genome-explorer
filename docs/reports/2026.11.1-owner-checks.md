# Lungfish 2026.11.1 owner check list

This is the owner check list of Preview 2026.11.1. It holds the rows that need a person's eyes, ears or hands and that no automated test covers. They are the five owner rows of the walk sheet (`SCR/walk/SHEET.md`: RM-10, RM-11, QL-SOUND, AIQUOTA and FEEL) and three rows that reviews of this session name (the Not specific badge for VoiceOver, the BLAST contact address with its consent line, and the one real send of BL-SMOKE). The other 99 rows of the sheet run by computer use or in Terminal and are in the walk record of the technical notes (`docs/reports/2026.11.1-technical.md`, section The publish and the walk). Each row is one task, and a row that a test comes to cover is deleted. The walk sheet's rule is that owner rows do not block the Preview. A failed row is a finding with a severity, and an open S1 or S2 finding blocks the next Stable release. Every permission that the walk needs, such as computer use, the Finder grant or a privacy prompt, is the owner's own click.

`SCR` is `/Users/dho/lungfish-phase-records/phase-3-2`. The owner works through VNC and hears no audio (O116), so a row about sound or VoiceOver audio waits for the owner at the Mac Studio itself, and the manager otherwise reads VoiceOver's caption panel and says so. The walk needs an unlocked screen. Severity follows `docs/contracts/SCOPE-AND-DELIVERY.md`. S1 is a false number, a silently wrong result, silent data loss or a crash. S2 is a common task that fails or a failure that shows no words. S3 is a rare task that fails or words that mislead. S4 is appearance.

Work on a copy of a project, never on the only copy of a result. A row that sends reads to NCBI starts only on the owner's go in chat or on the standing go's named list (O126).
