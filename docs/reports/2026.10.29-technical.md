## Known limits

The limits that still hold at 2026.10.28 are in the Known limits table of `docs/reports/2026.10.28-technical.md`. This Stable changes these.

| Area | What changed | State |
|---|---|---|
| NAO-MGS share | The share is computed in mates, in the unit of the taxon table, from `taxon_summaries.hit_count`, so a result on disk is right when it opens, with no rebuild. `manifest.hitCount` stays a row count. The lane's own limit is that a share with no database open shows 0.0% | closed |
| NAO-MGS BLAST Verify | The search covers all of `core_nt` and judges by tax ID, so it can take longer. A best match filed under a subtype, serotype or strain whose name lacks the taxon's, under the parent species or under the species above it counts as another organism. TaxTriage, NVD and Kraken2 have the same gap below species level (BLAST-CLADE-BY-LINEAGE, S3, trigger in the ruling) | closed for the S1, new limit |
| EsViritu BLAST | EsViritu passes no tax IDs, so a first-word rule judges, and "Rotavirus C" or any "Human ..." virus can count as supporting (BLAST-ESVIRITU-FIRST-WORD, S3, trigger a saved result whose supporting top hit lies in another species) | open, S3 |
| Column tips | The shared fix keeps a column's own tip. The NAOMGS-SHARE-BIO review lists two tips whose words are off (S3). They are the RPKMF tip of the EsViritu batch table, which says fragments where the single table says filtered reads and which the review did not confirm is reached, and the Kraken2 Reads tip, which should name read pairs for paired-end input (CLASSIFIER-READ-UNIT-WORDS) | new |
| Alerts | The NAO-MGS, NVD and CZ-ID load alerts use the failure presenter. The viewers' extraction and blocking alerts (about 20 at 2026.10.28) and a few unmoved sites still show the technical error as their text (S3) | changed |
