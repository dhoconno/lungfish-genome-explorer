## S1 and S2 results

Eight S1 rows of Demultiplex Barcodes with Cutadapt close, and one of them, DEMUX-DUAL-OFFSET-SHIFT, was in no published build. BIO rated each (DN1:78-83, DN2:131-161). The 5.4 lanes close no S1 or S2.

- DEMUX-DUAL-UNANCHORED. A custom dual-index file at Both Ends with no sample sheet was searched unanchored and placed 8.1% and 11.3% of real reads with no such barcodes in samples at error 0, and 74.0% and 82.2% at 0.15. The lines are now `^N{a}i7...i5N{b}$` and `^N{a}rc(i5)...rc(i7)N{b}$` for every a up to the 5' Distance and every b up to the 3' Distance, so the floor measures a null and the refusal of 2026.11.12 lifts for this route. 96 pairs of 8 and 10 bases placed 1 read in 54,000 single reads and 0 of 21,000 fragments. A search of more than 6,000 lines is refused (`4cff86fa5`, `2297d1871`). Kind `fastqDemultiplexDualSearchedAnywhere`, last build 13672.
- DEMUX-DUAL-BARE-REVERSE. At 5' End or 3' End a custom dual definition searched one barcode of each pair anywhere and wrote its reverse as the swapped pair. Cutadapt 5.2 left 10 of 10 reverse-strand reads unassigned. On 1,000 amplicon reads, a random 96-pair kit of 8 bases at 3' End and error 0.15 placed none with the old lines, but the same route with its reverse line corrected put 263 of the 1,000 reads in one sample by one chance match. The shape is the same, so BIO refused the location and did not repair the lines (DN2:36, DN2:133). Preflight now refuses the setup (`713276a94`). Kind `fastqDemultiplexDualOneEnd`, last build 13764.
- DEMUX-DUAL-FORM. At Both Ends a file in the i5 primer's form placed 0 of 40 reads, and two rows with one first barcode and reverse-complement second barcodes swapped their reads, 40 of 40 at error 0 and 0.15, and the lines of every build up to 2026.11.11 put 184 of 480 reads of the 12-row layout in another row at error 0 and 391 of 480 at 0.15. The run now chooses the form from the first 10,000 reads and refuses a mixed or undecided file (`95021de8b`). Kind `fastqDemultiplexDualFormSwap`, last build 13764.
- DEMUX-SINGLE-OFFSET-SHIFT, DEMUX-LINKED-OFFSET-SHIFT, DEMUX-OFFSET-PAST-DISTANCE and DEMUX-DUAL-OFFSET-SHIFT. Cutadapt 5.2 ranks lines by score and counts each N of an offset as a match, so a line of another row further in outscored the right one. TruSeq Single Index Set A (real flank) at 5' Distance 8 and error 0.15 put 14 of 216 reads in another sample, TruSeq HT Dual Index (real flanks) at 8/8 and error 0.15 put 112 of 1,152, and Nextera XT v2 (random flanks) at 3/3 put 14 of 672. The first two lived in Stable 2026.11.7, and their runs at 0/0 were clean of this shift (DEMUX-OFFSET-PAST-DISTANCE at 0/0, the Known limit below, is another fault). The fix checks every placed read and sends a tie, and a read that another row fits as well or better up to 4 bases past a distance, to Unassigned. The look-ahead left 0 wrong reads in the 60 cells of BIO's model. The check costs 31.2 s on 1 M records of the dual route (11.6% of a Cutadapt pass) and 8.4 s per 100,000 reads of the k96 route, with the time of the lane head `c92fdba0d`, landed as `6ef0a363d`. DEMUX-DUAL-OFFSET-SHIFT is the same fault on the anchored lines (2, 6 and 13 of 480 reads in another row in three runs at 3/3, 21 wrong reads in all) and no tag holds it.
- DEMUX-SHARED-NAME-GZIP. A run that wrote two lines under one name to gzip temporaries lost every read of an output past about 202 KB, exited 0 and counted only the whole outputs. A built-in dual kit at Both Ends 0/0 lost 100% of 50,000 reads, and a single-index kit at a distance above 0 reported 100.0% assigned with 11 of 12 samples empty. The fix (`794ae5bff`) writes plain temporaries when a row has two lines and gzips them at the end. It lived in Stable 2026.11.7 and could not wait for the next stable release (BIO).

Six AFFECTED kinds and rows join the table. The three offset and gzip rows have first build 4192 (2026.8.1), last build 13764, fix commits `ce890d4a4`, `ce890d4a4` and `794ae5bff`, and the what to do "Run it again in this version" (DN2:160).
