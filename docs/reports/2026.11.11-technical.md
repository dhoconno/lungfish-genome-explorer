## Limits added

- `rnaseq quant --write-plan --star-multimap-nmax <n>` takes a whole number from 1. It is refused in words with `salmon` (the default chain, which runs no STAR), `star-salmon` and `star-salmon-errormodel` (which map at nf-core/rnaseq's 20 loci), and without `--write-plan` (`SCR/lanes/EX-PLAN-STAR-M4.md:16-23`). The value 1000 matched the six measured runs of C6's deposit, and a larger limit changes the exon counts of the featureCounts chain.
- A sample sheet with a `kit` column plans the UMI branch of one preset, `quartz-seq2-x910`. A kit for some rows only, `--chain`, `--trim`, `--k`, `--decoys` and an unknown kit are refused in words and write no folder. STAR runs with `--readNameSeparator none`, so each read keeps its whole first word with the UMI at its end, and a pair whose mates end in `/1` and `/2` fails the layout check in words. The job asks for 1.25 times the sum of STAR's measured peak at its pairs, 8,000,000,000 B and 768 MiB for each thread, rounded up to a whole GiB. That is an upper bound, since samtools runs after STAR ends, and the sum also leaves room for `umi_tools count`, which nobody has measured (`SCR/lanes/EX-UMI.md:138-140,261-268`, `Sources/LungfishWorkflow/RNASeq/UMIPlanSubmit.swift:8-12`).
- `import rnaseq --adopt` of the `star-salmon` chain, STAR and then salmon, lists the files it leaves out and refuses a batch of two alignment scorings in words. Of the UMI branch it reads `umi/counts.tsv` in the unit `umis`, takes the table's ids plus the set's genes the table does not list, refuses a table that lists no gene and refuses a sample whose layout check failed (`SCR/lanes/EX-ADOPT-M6.md:32-44`).

The STAR index and the STARsolo adopt carry more limits.

- `rnaseq index-plan --genomeSAsparseD` takes a whole number from 1, refuses 1 without `--request-memory-mib`, and keeps STAR's `--limitGenomeGenerateRAM 32000000000` fixed. The request figures were measured at sparsity 2. The fixed limit is not a cap on use, since the full suffix array of GSE304256 (STAR 2.7.3a, Mmul_10) was built under the same limit and peaked at 36.6 GB resident (`SCR/lanes/REFS-5-1-E.md:31`, `SCR/lanes/REPRO-gse304256.md:97`). BIO gives the first run of the plan at sparsity 1 to IDX-C6 and IDX-C2 (`SCR/rulings/BIO-SOLO-M4.md` What binds 7, `SCR/lanes/SOLO-PLAN-M4.md:28,40`). On a 30 Mb slice with 2 million reads, sparsity 1 against 2 differed in 28,357 reads and 279 gene rows, and sparsity 1 against its repeat in none (`SCR/rulings/BIO-SOLO-M4.md:73`).
- `gencode-2020-A` is rule version 2, `tenx2-gencode-2020-A` in the folder and the key. It keeps a gene when one of its transcript lines has a `gene_type` and `transcript_type` on the list and neither the `readthrough_transcript` tag nor the `PAR` tag. On GENCODE v32 it keeps 36,601 of 60,668 genes and leaves out 24,067 (`docs/formats/reference-set.md:117`). It keeps GENCODE's versioned ids. A set with no `gene_type` is refused, and `index-adopt` refuses a run that read the file of rule version 1 in words that name both versions (`SCR/rulings/BIO-SOLO-M4.md` What binds 2, 4 and 5).
- The STARsolo adopt still states its multimapper line on every result though only the exon matrix is adopted, and an adopted hgmm result states that its genomes are not named (S3 rows SOLO-ADOPT-MULTIMAPPER-LINE-EXON-ONLY and SOLO-ADOPT-HGMM-GENOMES-NOT-NAMED).
