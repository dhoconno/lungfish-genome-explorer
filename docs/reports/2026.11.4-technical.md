### DEMUX-FLOOR-DECOY-MAX, an S1 by effect with rare reach, now a Known limit that the app reports in words

BIO's ruling (`SCR/rulings/BIO-DECOY-MAX-STABLE.md`, 48 calls) reads `VERDICT S1 BLOCKS-STABLE`. A barcode beats the chance floor when its reads exceed a binomial ceiling at tail `1e-6 / K`, and the share behind that ceiling is the larger of the uniform share and the margin times the composition share. The margin is the largest observed-to-expected ratio over about `max(1,000, 100 x K)` decoys. It is an empirical maximum over D decoys, so a barcode that is not in the pool, and whose starts are enriched as much as a decoy's could be, beats it about 1 time in D + 1, which is about 1 in 1,000 for each barcode. The kit tail of 1e-6 governs only the binomial step. When a barcode beats the floor its file is published as a sample, and before this round nothing the person saw marked it. The summary printed only the barcodes within chance, and the Operations row showed the floor's line only when the record named a barcode within chance. Rare reach does not lower the severity and no vote does, so the open row blocked the next stable release (`docs/contracts/SCOPE-AND-DELIVERY.md:64`).

The reach is wider than the row said. The dialog's default engine is Cutadapt at error rate 0.15 (`Sources/LungfishApp/Views/FASTQ/FASTQOperationDialogState.swift`), which gives one error to every barcode of 7 to 13 bases. So one error is the default for every built-in Illumina-family kit (8 bases) and for custom kits of 10 and 12 bases, and not a chosen setting. The default kit carries the bound too, at a lower measured rate. Exact Bare Barcode has no floor (its own row, DEMUX-EXACT-BARE-NO-FLOOR), and the Read Names route does not use the decoy floor.

| Kit and error setting | Reads | Per-barcode rate of held-out decoys | Real null cuts that publish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Default Illumina kit (12 barcodes of 8 bases), Cutadapt at 0.15 | 1,500 to 10^5 | 0.003 to 0.008% | 0 of 147 |
| Default Illumina kit, same setting | over 200,000 unassigned | mean 0.018%, worst cut 0.083% | 0 of 27 for a kit past the limit |
| Custom, 12 barcodes of 10 bases, one error | over 200,000 unassigned | mean 0.034%, worst cut 0.333% | 0 of 147 |
| Custom, 12 barcodes of 12 bases, one error | over 200,000 unassigned | mean 0.037%, worst cut 0.333% | 3 of 147, all on SRR36291587 R2 (QIAseq amplicon mate 2) |
| Custom, 12 barcodes of 12 bases, no error | over 200,000 unassigned | mean 0.015%, worst cut 0.167% | 0 of 147 |

A run publishes a sample that is not in the pool at about K times the per-barcode rate, for K barcodes of the kit that are not in the pool. The default kit with all 12 barcodes absent, such as a file with no barcode in its bases, does so in about 0.04 to 0.1% of runs up to 10^5 reads and about 0.2% of runs past 200,000 unassigned reads. A custom 12-barcode kit at one error does so in about 0.4% of runs on average. An amplicon file is the measured worst case, because one barcode (CAACTGCAACAT) comes within one error of a read start that the file holds at high copy, and the run publishes it at every size measured. BIO measured nothing new for the ruling. It took the ceilings and margins from saved records of the made 12-base one-error kit on SRR36291587 R2 and the rates from the tables of the earlier lanes.

| Reads | Unassigned | Margin | CAACTGCAACAT reads | Its ceiling | Over the ceiling | Share of the reads |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,500 | 1,494 | 63.99 | 6 | 5 | 1.2 x | 0.40% |
| 10^4 | 9,964 | 103.64 | 36 | 14 | 2.6 x | 0.36% |
| 85,199 | 84,882 | 86.92 | 317 | 45 | 7.0 x | 0.37% |

The false sample holds about 0.4% of the reads at every size, which is the median need of a real lone sample, so nobody can tell it from a small real sample by its reads. It beats its ceiling by up to 7 times, so a band just above chance would miss it, and the line below is never narrowed. For paired reads the same bound holds by the code, and it was not measured on paired cuts. A moved or copied project, an export and a reopened project keep the false sample with no mark, since the record does not change. BIO did not check the order of the built-in registry behind the default kit, the read shares of the held-out decoys that beat the floor, or how often the line shows on real pools.

The floor change that would close the row needs about 10^6 x K decoys for each run or a fitted tail of the decoy ratios, and amplicon files need decoys that model the starts they hold at high copy. That is a study with its own measure and a later-phase BIO row. This round took the smallest change, which is words. DECOYWORDS (`dfaa4467c` the red tests, `56444b9df` the words) puts one line in three places, namely `DemultiplexChanceWords.notInPoolLimit(publishedBarcodes:)` in `Sources/LungfishWorkflow/Demultiplex/DemultiplexChanceWords.swift`, the summary of `lungfish-cli fastq demultiplex` in `Sources/LungfishCLI/Commands/FastqDemultiplexSubcommand+Summary.swift`, and the Operations row with its Log line through `Sources/LungfishApp/Services/FASTQDemultiplexChanceNotice+Floor.swift`. The condition is a run whose chance floor ran and made at least one sample file that the floor passed, and the seam reads the record's `chanceFloor` block alone, which a record holds only when a barcode beat the floor (row D of the tally). UX set the words, byte for byte as landed.
