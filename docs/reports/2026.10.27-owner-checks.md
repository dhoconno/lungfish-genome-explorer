# Lungfish 2026.10.27 owner check list

This is the owner check list of Stable 2026.10.27, which promotes Preview 2026.10.26 (build 11593). It began as the check list of Preview 2026.10.25 and holds at most 20 rows still to run. Each row is one task that no automated test covers, and a row that a test comes to cover is deleted. A failed row is a finding with a severity, and an open S1 or S2 finding blocks the Stable release. Every permission that the walk needs, such as computer use, the Finder grant or a privacy prompt, is the owner's own click.

The re-walk of 2026-10-27 ran every row of the 2026.10.25 list, on candidate `9478bd939` (build 11575) and, for the Illumina kit row, row 17 and obs-22, on the published build 11584. The owner checks that evening ran more steps on 11584, DEMUX-STATS W3 and W4a, the last two panels of save-panel row 7 and Reassemble R2 steps 4 and 6. W4a found the S1 that Preview 2026.10.26 fixes (DEMUX-STATS-BEHIND), and its re-walk on the packaged 2026.10.26 (build 11593, `7d7f8f63b`) passed later that day. The steps that the re-walks did not run, or ran only in part, stay under Checks still to run with only what is left.
