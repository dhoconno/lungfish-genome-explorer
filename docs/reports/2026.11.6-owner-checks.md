# Lungfish 2026.11.6 owner check list

This is the owner check list of Preview 2026.11.6. It holds the checks that need a person's eyes or hands, or a walk of the packaged build that no automated test covers, for the changes since Preview 2026.11.5 and for the walk list of the Phase 4 plan (`docs/plans/2026-11-09-phase-4.md`, section The walk). It is written new for this Preview. The rows that the 2026.11.5 list left open stay on that list (`docs/reports/2026.11.5-owner-checks.md`) and are not repeated here. Each row is one task of about two minutes, and a row that a test comes to cover is deleted. Owner rows do not block the Preview. A failed row is a finding with a severity, and an open S1 or S2 finding blocks the next stable release. Severity follows `docs/contracts/SCOPE-AND-DELIVERY.md`. S1 is a false number, a silently wrong result, silent data loss or a crash. S2 is a common task that fails or a failure that shows no words. S3 is a rare task that fails or words that mislead. S4 is appearance.

`SCR` is `/Users/dho/lungfish-phase-records/phase-4`. The manager walks the packaged Preview by computer use, in background control unless the owner grants full-screen control, and walks the same rows on a Debug build first. The walk copy is installed and run outside `~/Documents` (`~/LGE-GUI-Walk/<date>/candidate`), and the walk needs an unlocked screen. Work on a copy of a project, never on the only copy of a result. Rows 10 and 15 to 17 need a person's own eyes, and row 19 needs a real pointer, so they say so in their Who column. Every permission that the walk needs, such as computer use, the Finder grant or a privacy prompt, is the owner's own click.

Row 12 sends a BLAST search to the recorded-reply stand-in on 127.0.0.1 only, so nothing reaches NCBI. A row that sends reads to NCBI starts only on the owner's go in chat or on the standing go's named list (O126), and no BLAST probe runs.

The notes of 2026.11.6 (`docs/release-notes/2026.11.6.md`) carry ten lines under What's new, one under What works differently and nine under Things to know. The table What each notes line rests on says which tests and rows each line stands on. The manager's walk confirms the claims of the notes before the publish, and a claim that the walk does not confirm comes out of the notes. Four claims rest on a reading of the code and not on a run, and rows 2, 8, 11 and 18 are the walk of them: the limit of the mapped contig list, the no-note part of Map and Assemble, the Completed with Warnings of a figure export, and the Log line of the Extract Reads sentence.
