## AFFECTED-STRIP-NO-WORDS (S2)

The fault. The main window draws its content under the title bar and unified toolbar (`MainWindowController.swift`, `.fullSizeContentView`). The host pins a surface that does not ask for the safe area to the viewer's top edge, and such a surface keeps its own content below the bar. The strip took that edge, so the bar covered every word and left a 1 to 2 pt peach line. On build 13190 the strip's frame read y 31 to 85 against a window top of 31, its label held all three lines of words (44 pt), and the EsViritu view began at 85 (`JOB notes/DIAGNOSIS.md`, read by accessibility on a clone of the walk project). The window's height played no part. Line 48 of the 2026.11.8 notes promised the note.

The fix. The strip starts at the container's safe area (`ViewerViewController+AffectedResultsStrip.swift`). Code reading and the SWIFT and UX reviews found three more ways to lose the words that the walk did not show. A notice that came before the next surface left the strip under it, one that came while no surface was installed or before a display that clears first was dropped, and a strip could stay over the next result until its record loaded. `ViewportHost.activeViewportDidChange` now says when a surface is installed or a clear leaves none. The viewer keeps the words of the latest notice and gives them to each installed surface. The Provenance model's `clear()` sends an empty notice, so the words end at each selection. A click on a classifier detection loads the record of its alignment evidence, and that load no longer speaks for the strip (`speaksForViewer`), so the strip of the result on screen stays. The strip counts further notes "under Warnings on the Inspector's Provenance tab".

Each test was red first. They read the strip's words, a height of more than one line and a place below the title bar in a window that draws under it (red read 694 pt against 668 pt), a notice before the next surface, with no surface installed and before a display that clears first, the empty notice of a clear, the evidence load that leaves the strip, and the Provenance tab sentence. The host has its own test. Green on `3c5c73665`, 118 cases with the classifier alignment suites (`JOB lanes/LANE-FIX.md`, `red-2.log`, `green-2.log`). Reviews `JOB reviews/FIX-STRIP-SWIFT.md` and `FIX-STRIP-UX.md` asked for changes with no blocker, applied in `ebf63121d` (tests, red), `3c5c73665` and `922fb26cd` (green). On the delta UX said MERGE. SWIFT asked for a test of the flag's reset (done) and for the strip to say that a click on a detection replaces the Provenance tab. UX, who rules words, kept the strip's sentence, and the notes say it under Things to know instead.
