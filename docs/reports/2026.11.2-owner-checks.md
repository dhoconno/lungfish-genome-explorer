# Lungfish 2026.11.2 owner check list

This is the owner check list of Preview 2026.11.2. It holds the checks that need a person's eyes, ears or hands, or a walk of the packaged build that no automated test covers. Rows 1 to 7 are the rows of `docs/reports/2026.11.1-owner-checks.md` that are still open. Rows 8 to 26 are the walk rows that walk 2 of Preview 2026.11.1 left (`SCR/walk/walk-log.md` section Walk 2 close, `SCR/CONTINUE-3.2.md` step 4). Rows 27 to 34 are the walk rows that the session 2 reviews and lane reports name, and rows 35 to 37 are the unrated observations of walk 3 (`SCR/walk/walk-log.md`, section Walk 3). Each row is one task, and a row that a test comes to cover is deleted. Owner rows do not block the Preview. A failed row is a finding with a severity, and an open S1 or S2 finding blocks the next Stable release. Every permission that the walk needs, such as computer use, the Finder grant or a privacy prompt, is the owner's own click.

`SCR` is `/Users/dho/lungfish-phase-records/phase-3-2`. The owner works through VNC and hears no audio (O116), so a row about sound or VoiceOver audio waits for the owner at the Mac Studio itself, and the manager otherwise reads VoiceOver's caption panel and says so. Most of rows 8 to 34 need a context menu, a window set to an exact width or full-screen control, so the manager walks them by computer use with the owner at galah. The walk needs an unlocked screen, and the walk copy is installed and run outside `~/Documents` (`~/LGE-GUI-Walk/<date>/candidate`). Severity follows `docs/contracts/SCOPE-AND-DELIVERY.md`. S1 is a false number, a silently wrong result, silent data loss or a crash. S2 is a common task that fails or a failure that shows no words. S3 is a rare task that fails or words that mislead. S4 is appearance.

Work on a copy of a project, never on the only copy of a result. A row that sends reads to NCBI starts only on the owner's go in chat or on the standing go's named list (O126), and no BLAST probe runs.
