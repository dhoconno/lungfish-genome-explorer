# Your first project

Filmed with Lungfish 2026.9.72. The narration is a synthetic voice.

Choose Help, then Demo Projects. Demo projects are ready-made practice projects built from public sequencing data. Find Human Mapping and Variants, with results, and click Download and Open. You need an internet connection.

A project is one folder on your Mac. The sidebar on the left shows what it holds. Imports holds the reads. A read is the sequence of bases that a sequencer reports for one short DNA fragment.

Click HG002 under Imports. HG002 is a Genome in a Bottle reference sample, from a donor who agreed to share their genome publicly. The Inspector on the right shows how many reads there are and a summary of their base quality.

Results go under Analyses, and these were made in advance. Open the minimap2 result. minimap2 is a read aligner. It finds where each read best fits the reference, a 500 kb slice of human chromosome 20. Zoom in to see each read drawn at its aligned position.

Click Operations in the toolbar. The Operations Panel lists each long task LGE runs, like this download, with its progress and whether it finished.

To learn more about projects, read the manual chapter The Lungfish Project. More videos are on the Videos page of the website.
