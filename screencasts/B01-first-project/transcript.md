# Your first project

Filmed with Lungfish Preview 2026.9.75. The narration is a synthetic voice.

Choose Help, then Demo Projects. Each one holds real public data. Find Human Mapping and Variants, with results, and click Download and Open. You need an internet connection.

A project is one folder on your Mac. The sidebar on the left shows what is inside. Imports holds the reads, short pieces of DNA read by a sequencer.

Click HG002 under Imports. These are reads from one anonymous human volunteer. The Inspector on the right shows how many reads there are and their quality scores.

Results go under Analyses, and these were made in advance. Open the minimap2 result and zoom in. Each read sits where it matches the reference, a slice of human chromosome 20.

Click Operations in the toolbar. The Operations Panel lists every task the app runs, like this download, and whether it finished.

To learn more about projects, read the manual chapter The Lungfish Project.
