# Judge a sequencing run

Filmed with Lungfish 2026.9.72. The narration is a synthetic voice.

This video uses the Human Reads demo project. Choose Help, then Demo Projects, and click Download and Open beside Human Reads. The video Your first project shows each step.

Click the HG002 reads under Imports. HG002 is a well studied human sample. The cards across the top sum up all the reads in this file, how many there are, how long they are, and how good their bases are.

Each base gets a quality score, the sequencer's own estimate of error, written Q. Q20 means one wrong base in a hundred. Q30 means one in a thousand.

Here 91 percent of bases reach Q30. For Illumina, the most common short read sequencer, a rule of thumb calls above 80 percent healthy.

Click the Q / Position chart to open it. Quality falls toward the end of each read, which is normal for this kind of sequencing. Here it stays above Q20. Click outside the chart to close it.

Length Dist. peaks at 250 bases, the length the run was set to make. GC content, the share of bases that are G or C, is 39 percent, close to the human average of about 41.
